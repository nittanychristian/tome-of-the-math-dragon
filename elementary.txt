Elementary curriculum — practice scope

Baseline: typical US grade progression using the Common Core mathematics content
strands (https://www.thecorestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/Math_Standards.pdf).
These chapters provide generated practice and worked hints. They supplement
instruction; they do not claim assessment or certification of every standard.
Each bullet is a reachable quest with one or more implemented generators.
Grade 2 includes requested early multiplication practice beyond the baseline:
six fixed-first-factor tables (0–5), with second factors 0–9 and equal-group visuals.

Continuous practice
Choose a grade and chapter, turn CONTINUOUS PRACTICE on, then choose any single
quest. Questions stay in that quest without completing it. Turn it off to finish
on the next correct answer after reaching the quest target, or leave via the map.
For single-digit times tables: Grade 3 -> Single-Digit Times Tables. Both factors
are 0–9. Continuous mode also works for existing middle school quests. Bosses
always retain their normal victory conditions. This preference lasts for the
current session; passcodes record chapter progress, not the practice toggle.
